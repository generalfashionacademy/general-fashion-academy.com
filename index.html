Our History

Founded in the early 1990s by Mr.Osei Asibey Bonsu in Kwadaso, Kumasi, General Fashion Academy (GFA) was established to bridge the gap between traditional informal apprenticeship and structured, professional fashion education in the Ashanti Region. Beginning as a specialized tailoring enterprise recognized for high-precision church vestments—such as cassocks, albs, clerical shirts, chasubles, and choir robes alongside custom ladies’ and gentlemen’s wear, the academy built an early reputation for technical precision. As Kumasi grew into a vibrant fashion hub, GFA expanded into a full-scale educational institute to equip aspiring designers with pattern drafting, freehand cutting, garment assembly, and fashion entrepreneurship skills. Over the past three decades, GFA has evolved into a premier incubator for dressmakers and tailors by combining intensive 98% practical workshop training with modernized digital administration systems, allowing students across Ghana to easily access admissions, manage coursework, and master both traditional Ghanaian garment construction and contemporary fashion design.
